Errata for BLFS Version 11.1

  • Phonon installs plugins in /usr/lib, not /usr/lib/qt5 and the QT_PLUGIN_PATH for kf5 was therefore wrong. This probably only affects old ex-qt4 packages from 'Further KDE5 packages' such as the dragon video player. For the correct QT_PLUGIN_PATH etc see the development book.
