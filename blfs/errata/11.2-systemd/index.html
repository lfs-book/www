Errata for BLFS systemd Version 11.2

  • The dependencies as published for libreoffice were incorrect. Libreoffice no longer needs neon, serf, or apr - but needs libwebp and libtiff to be installed.
  • Grilo is missing a dependency on libsoup3, which is necessary to compile the package.
  • The option to enable ALSA in Thunderbird was incorrect. To resolve this (if you want ALSA only), replace --disable-pulseaudio and --enable-alsa with --enable-audio-backends=alsa.
  • In the "Configuring" section of libpwquality, "gecoscheck" is misspelled as "geoscheck".
  • In the dependencies of Exim-4.96, "PCRE-8.45" was incorrect. It should be "PCRE2-10.40".
