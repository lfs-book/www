What is BLFS?

Beyond Linux From Scratch (BLFS) is a project that aims to assist users from where LFS left off. It endeavors to assist users to develop their systems in line with their needs by providing a broad range of instructions for installing and configuring various packages on top of a base LFS system.

If you are wondering why you would want an LFS system or what one of them is, then you don't want to be here for now - you should head over to the LFS Project Homepage where all will be explained.

We hope you find BLFS useful and enjoy getting your system customized just the way you want.
