New BLFS Editor | Bruce Dubbs - 2005/06/13

The BLFS project announces that David Jensen is the latest addition to the BLFS Editing Team. He is a long time participant of the BLFS newsgroups and and brings important skills and new enthusiasm to the team.

BLFS 6.0 Release | Bruce Dubbs - 2005/05/02

The BLFS Development Team is pleased to announce the release of BLFS 6.0. Version 6.0 is a major milestone in the evolution of BLFS. It is specifically designed to build upon LFS 6.0. This version provides installation instructions for 357 packages and an additional 21 sections covering configuration and customization of different aspects of your system.
