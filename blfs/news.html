BLFS News

BLFS 7.4 has been released!
Bruce Dubbs - 2013/09/13

After five years, The BLFS Team is happy to present version 7.4 of Beyond Linux From Scratch. This version includes approximately 750 packages beyond the base Linux From Scratch Version 7.4 book. You can view the 7.4 version on your local mirror.

BLFS 6.3-rc3 has been released!
Randy McMurchy - 2008/08/12

The BLFS Development team is pleased to announce the release of the 6.3-rc3 version of BLFS. This is a release candidate of the 6.3 version, due to be released on August 24th. You can view the 6.3-rc3 version on your local mirror. For additional information about the release, along with other download and format options, see the 6.3 Release Notes.
