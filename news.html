LFS Beta Website Announced
Justin Knierim - 2005/07/04

Over the past few months, the LFS website team has been redesigning the LFS website. One main goal of this redesign was to simplify the structure and navigation of the website, creating a site that is easy to use and easy to maintain. At this point we are presenting the site to the entire community for feedback and comments.

Please feel free to review this site and give any feedback or comments to the website mailing list. Thank you.
